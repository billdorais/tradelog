Why entries were blocked loading…
Signal-level. A signal records as blocked only when EVERY target account was dropped, so gates that stop one book while another still trades are invisible here. That under-counts account-specific gates (hours, RVOL, side, reversal, halts) and over-weights whole-signal ones (day-type, which blocks the farms too). For “why didn’t THIS book trade?” use the per-account panel below.
