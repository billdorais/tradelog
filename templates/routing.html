◆ Buying-Power Pause — stop new entries at high utilization
Pause at utilization (%) per account · exposure ÷ total buying power Disabled

◆ Day-Type Gate — breakouts only on Outside days
Block breakout entries on Inside/Neutral days All three paper books (Paper All, Refined, Kairos) Disabled
From the inside/outside-day test: breakouts only pay on "Outside" days (narrow prior-day CPR → expansion). When on, breakout entries are skipped on Inside/Neutral days for all three paper books (Paper All now runs the Kairos engine entries too). Reversals are gated separately below. Fails open if a ticker can't be classified.
Block reversal entries on Outside/Neutral days Paper All (acct1) + Kairos (acct3) only · Refined left alone Disabled
Inside-day → reversals thesis. ⚠ Weaker than the breakout gate: in the test, R3S3 reversals win on Outside days too, so this can prune good trades — left OFF by default, enable to test. When on, reversal entries fire only on Inside days for Paper All + Kairos (Refined stays TV-driven and ungated). Fails open if a ticker can't be classified.
