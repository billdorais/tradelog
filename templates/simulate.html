Max-Loss Stop Test — of your real trades, how many dipped past a hard $ stop then recovered and closed higher? (Maximum Adverse Excursion)
Real round-trips · MAE from 1-min bars · uses From/To above.
Recovered = touched the stop then closed higher (the stop's cost). Bled = touched it and closed at/below (the stop's saving). Net Δ = book P&L with the stop minus without — positive means the stop would have helped. Stop fills are modeled at the level (no slippage haircut).

Gap-Fill → Prior-Day VWAP Backtest — buy the morning fade after a gap, target the prior-day VWAP
Uses the From/To dates above. Read-only — places no orders. “Gap” = any opening gap ≥ threshold (no earnings calendar, so this is a proxy for the earnings-gap population). Long-only first pass: after the warmup chop, buy when price is below the prior-day VWAP with enough room, stop under the early-session low, target the prior-day VWAP. Fetching intraday bars for several tickers can take 10–30s.
Earnings Reaction Study — what each stock actually did the morning after earnings, ~6 years back
Ignores the From/To dates — it enumerates each ticker’s actual earnings dates and measures the next session directly, so the sample is ~24 events per ticker instead of whatever falls inside a date window. Daily bars: Yahoo keeps only 59 days of intraday history, so multi-year earnings work cannot be 5m. “Filled” = price traded back through the prior close that session. “Faded” = closed against the gap direction (measured from the open, where you could actually enter). Fetching several tickers can take 10–30s.
