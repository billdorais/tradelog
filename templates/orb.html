Live trading · Crew Live (account 6) — REAL MONEY
The same rules with real money on Crew Live, beside the crew's Camarilla rules: a bracket order (range stop, 3R target) when a 5-minute candle closes outside the range, flat at 15:55. It only trades tickers no crew rule trades, and at 15:55 it closes only its own positions. Each trade is sized to the risk below, then sized down if needed to fit the live position limit (LIVE_MAX_POSITION_PCT of equity, and the buying power). Kairos' trailing stops and max holds leave these trades alone; Crew Live's profit lock, daily loss guard and 15:58 close still apply. Dry run logs the orders it would send and sends nothing.
