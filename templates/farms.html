How to read this
Both farms trade every pipeline at an equal $1,000 a trade, so their numbers compare directly. Each farm's strategies are ranked on net P&L over the window (net-positive, with the min-trades floor and per-ticker cap above). The chart has three lines: today's top N by period — hindsight, they were picked because these numbers are good — and the line that matters, the forward pick: at each period boundary the top N is re-ranked on only what was known then, and the line is what those picks made the next period. The dashed line is what N average farm strategies made. If the forward line doesn't clear the dashed one, picking winners isn't working.
