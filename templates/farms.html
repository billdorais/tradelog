How to read this
Both farms trade every pipeline at an equal $1,000 a trade, so their numbers compare directly. Each farm's strategies are ranked on net P&L over the window (net-positive, with the min-trades floor and per-ticker cap above). The chart has three lines: today's top N by period — hindsight, they were picked because these numbers are good — and the line that matters, the forward pick: at each period boundary the top N is re-ranked on only what was known then, and the line is what those picks made the next period. The dashed line is what N average farm strategies made. If the forward line doesn't clear the dashed one, picking winners isn't working.

Pick and hold: forward test a frozen roster
How you run Kairos Select: look back, take the top N, keep them. Choose a pick date. The top N (and the min-trades and per-ticker settings above) are ranked on the lookback before it and frozen. The grey part of the line is the audition: it looks good because that's why they were picked. The blue part is the test: what the frozen list did after the pick, against the same number of average strategies on the same farm. Save a pick to keep that exact list and watch it build a record.

Replay the roster — exit tests
Re-runs every roster pick's real trades from the farm it comes from on 1-minute bars, changing one exit setting at a time against your live exits (each rule's trail and trigger, its max hold or the global backstop). Both are simulated the same way, so the difference is the setting. Trades are split at the median date: the best setting is picked on the older half and then checked on the newer half — only trust a setting that wins on both. Farm trades are $1,000 each; read results as % per trade. Uses the roster and window chosen above.
